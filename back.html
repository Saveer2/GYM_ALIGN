Back Workouts

Pull-Ups

Primary Muscle Focus: Latissimus Dorsi

Purpose: Builds width and strength in upper back.

How to Do:

1. Grip a pull-up bar with hands slightly wider than shoulder-width.
2. Hang with arms fully extended, then pull your chest toward the bar until chin clears it.
3. Lower back to full extension under control.

Lat Pulldown

Primary Muscle Focus: Lats

Purpose: Alternative to pull-ups for lat development using a machine.

How to Do:

1. Sit at the lat pulldown machine and grasp the bar with a wide grip.
2. Pull the bar down to your upper chest while keeping torso upright.
3. Slowly release the bar back up to full arm extension.

Bent-Over Barbell Row

Primary Muscle Focus: Middle Back, Lats

Purpose: Builds thickness and strength in the back.

How to Do:

1. Stand with feet hip-width and hinge at the hips keeping back flat.
2. Grab the barbell with an overhand grip and pull it toward your lower chest/upper abdomen.
3. Lower under control and repeat.

Seated Cable Row

Primary Muscle Focus: Middle Back, Rhomboids

Purpose: Develops the mid-back and posture muscles.

How to Do:

1. Sit at the cable row station and place feet on the platform.
2. Grab the handle and pull it toward your torso while squeezing shoulder blades together.
3. Return to full arm extension under control.

Single-Arm Dumbbell Row

Primary Muscle Focus: Lats & Rhomboids

Purpose: Improves unilateral back strength and balance.

How to Do:

1. Place one knee and hand on a bench and hold a dumbbell with the other hand.
2. Row the dumbbell up to your waist by driving elbow up and back.
3. Lower with control and repeat for the other side.

T-Bar Row

Primary Muscle Focus: Middle Back

Purpose: Adds thickness and mass to the mid-back.

How to Do:

1. Stand over the T-bar, hinge at hips and keep back flat.
2. Pull the handle toward your chest while keeping elbows close to the body.
3. Lower slowly and repeat.

Deadlift

Primary Muscle Focus: Posterior Chain (Back, Glutes, Hamstrings)

Purpose: Full-body strength movement that strengthens the entire posterior chain.

How to Do:

1. Stand with barbell over mid-foot and feet hip-width apart.
2. Hinge at the hips, chest up, grip the bar, and lift by extending hips and knees simultaneously.
3. Lower the bar back down with control keeping a neutral spine.

Hyperextension (Back Extension)

Primary Muscle Focus: Erector Spinae (Lower Back)

Purpose: Strengthens lower back and spinal erectors.

How to Do:

1. Position yourself on a hyperextension bench with hips supported.
2. Lower your torso toward the floor by hinging at the hips.
3. Raise your torso back up until the body is straight and repeat.
