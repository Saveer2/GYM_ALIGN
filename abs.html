Abs Workouts

Crunches

Primary Muscle Focus: Rectus Abdominis (Upper Abs)

Purpose: Strengthens upper abdominal muscles.

How to Do:

1. Lie on your back with knees bent and feet flat.
2. Place hands behind your head or across your chest.
3. Curl your shoulders up toward the ceiling using your abs, then lower back down.

Leg Raises (Lying)

Primary Muscle Focus: Lower Abdominals

Purpose: Targets the lower portion of the abs.

1. Lie flat on your back with legs extended.
2. Keeping legs straight, lift them up until they're perpendicular to the floor.
3. Lower them slowly without touching the floor and repeat.

Plank

Primary Muscle Focus: Entire Core

Purpose: Builds core stability and endurance.

1. Get into a forearm plank position with body in a straight line.
2. Keep core tight and hold the position for the desired duration.

Russian Twist

Primary Muscle Focus: Obliques

Purpose: Develops rotational core strength and oblique definition.

1. Sit on the floor with knees bent and lean back slightly.
2. Hold a weight or medicine ball and rotate your torso to each side touching the weight near the floor.
3. Repeat alternating sides.

Mountain Climbers

Primary Muscle Focus: Core & Cardio

Purpose: Combines core activation with cardiovascular conditioning.

1. Start in a high plank position.
2. Drive one knee toward your chest, then quickly switch legs in a running motion.
3. Continue alternating at a steady pace.

Hanging Leg Raise

Primary Muscle Focus: Lower Abs & Hip Flexors

Purpose: Builds lower abs and hip flexor strength.

1. Hang from a pull-up bar with an overhand grip.
2. Keeping legs straight, raise them in front of you until hip height or higher.
3. Lower slowly back to the start.

Bicycle Crunches

Primary Muscle Focus: Obliques & Rectus Abdominis

Purpose: Targets both the rectus abdominis and obliques with rotational movement.

1. Lie on your back with hands behind your head and knees lifted.
2. Bring one elbow toward the opposite knee while extending the other leg, alternating sides in a pedaling motion.

Ab Wheel Rollout

Primary Muscle Focus: Core (Rectus & Spinal Stabilizers)

Purpose: Advanced move that challenges the entire core for strength and stability.

1. Kneel on the floor and hold the ab wheel handles.
2. Roll the wheel forward while keeping a braced core until arms are extended.
3. Roll back to the kneeling start position.
