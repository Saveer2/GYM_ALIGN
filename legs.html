Leg Workouts

Barbell Squat

Primary Muscle Focus: Quadriceps, Glutes, Hamstrings

Purpose: Develops lower body strength and power.

How to Do:

1. Position barbell across upper back and stand with feet shoulder-width apart.
2. Brace core and hinge hips back as you bend knees to lower into a squat.
3. Descend until thighs are parallel to the floor, then drive up through heels.

Leg Press

Primary Muscle Focus: Quadriceps, Glutes

Purpose: Heavy-load leg exercise using a machine for controlled motion.

How to Do:

1. Sit on the leg press machine and place feet shoulder-width on the platform.
2. Unrack the sled and lower the platform by bending knees.
3. Push the platform back to full extension without locking knees.

Lunges

Primary Muscle Focus: Glutes & Quads

Purpose: Improves unilateral leg strength and balance.

How to Do:

1. Stand tall and take a large step forward with one leg.
2. Lower the rear knee toward the floor until both knees are about 90°.
3. Push through the front heel to return to standing and repeat.

Leg Extension

Primary Muscle Focus: Quadriceps

Purpose: Isolates the quads for strengthening and definition.

How to Do:

1. Sit in the leg extension machine and hook ankles under the pad.
2. Extend your knees until legs are straight, pause briefly, then lower.

Leg Curl

Primary Muscle Focus: Hamstrings

Purpose: Targets the hamstring muscles.

How to Do:

1. Position yourself on the leg curl machine as instructed.
2. Curl your heels toward your glutes by bending the knees.
3. Lower the weight back down under control.

Calf Raise

Primary Muscle Focus: Gastrocnemius & Soleus

Purpose: Builds calf strength and size.

How to Do:

1. Stand on the edge of a step or calf block with heels hanging off.
2. Raise your heels as high as possible, hold briefly, then lower slowly below parallel.

Bulgarian Split Squat

Primary Muscle Focus: Quads & Glutes

Purpose: Single-leg exercise for balance and hypertrophy.

How to Do:

1. Stand a couple feet from a bench with one foot resting behind you.
2. Lower your hips by bending the front knee until the thigh is parallel to the floor.
3. Drive through the front heel to return to start.

Hack Squat

Primary Muscle Focus: Quadriceps

Purpose: Machine-based squat variation targeting the quads.

How to Do:

1. Position yourself on hack squat machine with back and shoulders against pads.
2. Lower by bending knees until thighs approach parallel, then press back up.
