Triceps Workouts

Tricep Pushdown (Cable)

Primary Muscle Focus: Triceps (All Heads)

Purpose: Isolates triceps with constant tension.

How to Do:

1. Attach a bar to the high pulley and grasp it with an overhand grip.
2. Keep elbows tucked and push the bar down until arms are fully extended.
3. Slowly return to the start position.

Overhead Dumbbell Extension

Primary Muscle Focus: Long Head of Triceps

Purpose: Stretches and strengthens the long head for size.

How to Do:

1. Sit or stand holding a dumbbell with both hands overhead.
2. Lower the dumbbell behind your head by bending elbows.
3. Extend the arms back to the top position.

Close-Grip Bench Press

Primary Muscle Focus: Triceps & Inner Chest

Purpose: Heavy compound move emphasizing triceps strength.

How to Do:

1. Lie on a bench and grip the bar about shoulder-width or narrower.
2. Lower the bar to your chest keeping elbows close to body.
3. Press up until arms are fully extended.

Dips (Parallel Bars)

Primary Muscle Focus: Triceps & Chest

Purpose: Bodyweight compound move for pressing strength.

How to Do:

1. Grip parallel bars and support your weight with straight arms.
2. Lower your body by bending elbows until about 90°.
3. Push back up to the starting position.

Skull Crushers

Primary Muscle Focus: Triceps

Purpose: Isolates triceps for mass and strength.

How to Do:

1. Lie on a flat bench holding an EZ bar or barbell with arms extended.
2. Bend elbows to lower the bar toward your forehead.
3. Extend arms back to the starting position.

Tricep Kickback

Primary Muscle Focus: Triceps (Lateral Head)

Purpose: Isolates the triceps for shaping and toning.

How to Do:

1. Hold a dumbbell, hinge at the hips keeping back flat.
2. Extend the working arm backward until straight, pause, then return.

Cable Overhead Extension

Primary Muscle Focus: Long Head of Triceps

Purpose: Provides constant tension while extending overhead.

How to Do:

1. Attach a rope to a low pulley and face away from the machine.
2. Grip the rope overhead and extend elbows until arms are straight.
3. Return slowly to starting position.

Diamond Push-Ups

Primary Muscle Focus: Triceps & Chest

Purpose: Bodyweight variation that emphasizes triceps.

How to Do:

1. Assume push-up position and place hands close together so thumbs and index fingers form a diamond.
2. Lower chest toward hands keeping elbows close to body.
3. Push back up to full arm extension.
