Shoulder Exercise Guide

Overhead Barbell Press

Focus: Deltoids (Front & Side)

Purpose: Builds overall shoulder size and strength.

  1. Stand with feet shoulder-width apart.
  2. Grip the barbell slightly wider than shoulders.
  3. Unrack the bar and position it at chest/shoulder level.
  4. Press overhead until arms are extended, then lower slowly.

Dumbbell Shoulder Press

Focus: Deltoids

Purpose: Improves shoulder stability and power.

  1. Sit upright on a bench with back support.
  2. Hold dumbbells at shoulder height.
  3. Press overhead until arms are extended, then lower slowly.

Lateral Raise

Focus: Lateral Deltoids

Purpose: Builds shoulder width and definition.

  1. Stand with dumbbells at sides, elbows slightly bent.
  2. Raise arms to shoulder height, pause, and lower slowly.

Front Raise

Focus: Anterior Deltoids

Purpose: Isolates and strengthens front shoulders.

  1. Stand holding dumbbells in front of thighs.
  2. Raise dumbbells to shoulder height and lower slowly.

Rear Delt Fly (Bent-Over)

Focus: Posterior Deltoids

Purpose: Develops rear shoulders for balance.

  1. Hinge at hips, flat back, dumbbells hanging down.
  2. Raise arms out to the sides, squeeze shoulder blades.

Arnold Press

Focus: All Heads of Deltoids

Purpose: Targets all shoulder heads, improves flexibility.

  1. Start with dumbbells at chest level, palms facing you.
  2. Rotate palms outward as you press overhead, then reverse.

Upright Row

Focus: Trapezius & Deltoids

Purpose: Builds traps and side shoulders.

  1. Hold a barbell with overhand grip.
  2. Pull toward upper chest, lead with elbows.

Dumbbell Shrugs

Focus: Trapezius

Purpose: Strengthens upper traps and neck muscles.

  1. Hold dumbbells at sides.
  2. Shrug shoulders up toward ears, hold, then lower slowly.
