Chest Workouts

Barbell Bench Press

Primary Muscle Focus: Pectoralis Major

Purpose: Builds mass and strength in middle chest.

How to Do:

1. Lie flat on a bench and plant feet firmly on the floor.
2. Grip the bar slightly wider than shoulder-width and unrack it.
3. Lower the bar to mid-chest under control.
4. Drive the bar up until arms are fully extended.

Incline Dumbbell Press

Primary Muscle Focus: Upper Chest

Purpose: Targets upper pecs for chest fullness.

How to Do:

1. Set an incline bench to 30–45° and sit with dumbbells at shoulder height.
2. Press the dumbbells up until arms are extended.
3. Lower slowly back to starting position.

Decline Bench Press

Primary Muscle Focus: Lower Chest

Purpose: Develops lower portion of chest.

How to Do:

1. Lie on a decline bench and secure feet.
2. Grip the bar and lower it to the lower chest.
3. Press the bar back up until arms are extended.

Dumbbell Fly

Primary Muscle Focus: Pectoralis Major

Purpose: Stretches and shapes the chest.

How to Do:

1. Lie on a flat bench holding dumbbells above chest with slight elbow bend.
2. Open arms wide in an arc until you feel a stretch in the chest.
3. Bring the dumbbells back together by squeezing the chest.

Push-Ups

Primary Muscle Focus: Chest, Shoulders, Triceps

Purpose: Bodyweight exercise for chest development and core stability.

How to Do:

1. Place hands slightly wider than shoulder-width and extend legs back into a plank.
2. Lower chest until it nearly touches the floor.
3. Push back up to full arm extension.

Cable Crossover

Primary Muscle Focus: Inner Chest

Purpose: Defines chest shape and inner pecs with constant tension.

How to Do:

1. Set pulleys to a high position and grab both handles.
2. Step forward slightly and pull handles together in front of chest.
3. Slowly return to the starting position.

Chest Dips

Primary Muscle Focus: Lower Chest & Triceps

Purpose: Emphasizes lower chest when leaning forward.

How to Do:

1. Grip parallel bars and support your weight with straight arms.
2. Lean forward slightly and lower until elbows reach about 90°.
3. Press back to the starting position.

Machine Chest Press

Primary Muscle Focus: Chest

Purpose: Controlled pressing motion for chest development.

How to Do:

1. Adjust seat and grips so handles are at chest level.
2. Push handles forward until arms are extended.
3. Return to start under control.
