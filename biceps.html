Biceps Workouts

Barbell Curl

Primary Muscle Focus: Biceps Brachii

Purpose: Builds overall biceps size and strength.

How to Do:

1. Hold a barbell with an underhand grip, shoulder-width apart.
2. Keep your elbows close to your sides and curl the bar upward.
3. Lower slowly to the starting position.

Dumbbell Curl

Primary Muscle Focus: Biceps

Purpose: Enhances muscle balance and peak development.

How to Do:

1. Hold a dumbbell in each hand with palms facing forward.
2. Curl both dumbbells upward while keeping elbows stationary.
3. Lower back down with control.

Hammer Curl

Primary Muscle Focus: Biceps & Brachialis

Purpose: Improves arm thickness and forearm strength.

How to Do:

1. Hold dumbbells with palms facing your torso.
2. Curl the weights up while maintaining a neutral grip.
3. Lower under control and repeat.

Concentration Curl

Primary Muscle Focus: Biceps Peak

Purpose: Isolates one bicep for better peak contraction.

How to Do:

1. Sit on a bench and rest your elbow on the inside of your thigh.
2. Curl the dumbbell upward, squeezing at the top.
3. Lower slowly and repeat.

Preacher Curl

Primary Muscle Focus: Biceps

Purpose: Provides strict isolation by preventing shoulder involvement.

How to Do:

1. Sit on a preacher bench with arms resting on the pad.
2. Hold the EZ bar and curl it up to shoulder height.
3. Lower it slowly back to the start position.

Cable Curl

Primary Muscle Focus: Biceps

Purpose: Provides constant tension on biceps throughout the movement.

How to Do:

1. Attach a straight bar to the low pulley of a cable machine.
2. Hold with an underhand grip and curl upward.
3. Lower slowly maintaining tension on biceps.

Chin-Up

Primary Muscle Focus: Biceps & Lats

Purpose: Bodyweight exercise that builds arm and back strength.

How to Do:

1. Grab the bar with an underhand grip, hands shoulder-width apart.
2. Pull your chin above the bar.
3. Lower back down fully and repeat.
